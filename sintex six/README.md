# 🚀 GOKU: SPACE MISSION COMMANDER
### মহাকাশ অভিযান: ড্রাগন ওডিসি (A Story-Driven Interplanetary Game)

An educational, story-driven space exploration web game inspired by the **Space Game Script PDF** and featuring **Commander Son Goku** (Capsule Corp × NASA Space Program). Fully responsive and optimized for both **Desktop computers** and **Smartphones / Tablets**.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Launch the Game**:
   - Simply double-click [`index.html`](index.html) to open in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, Opera, Brave).
   - No installation, Node.js, or web server required! Everything runs 100% offline.
2. **Controls**:
   - **Desktop**:
     - **Arrow Keys / W, A, S, D**: Steer, Throttle, Pitch, and Maneuver.
     - **Spacebar / E / Enter**: Action (Thruster burn, Sample scan & collect, Spacewalk EVA repair, Ki-Shield, Decrypt data).
   - **Mobile / Smartphone**:
     - On-screen touch D-Pad (▲, ▼, ◀, ▶) and large tactile **THRUST** & **ACTION** buttons.
     - Responsive glassmorphism cockpit dashboard that adapts to portrait and landscape modes.

  - **Mission 01: FIRST LAUNCH (প্রথম উৎক্ষেপণ)** (⭐) — Liftoff from Earth, throttle management, gravity turn pitch angle, and circularizing into stable 300 km orbit.

  - **Mission 03: SAMPLE HUNT (নমুনা সংগ্রহ)** (⭐⭐⭐) — Deploy rover / Goku on Flying Nimbus, scan the terrain, collect 5 scientific samples. Tradeoff: Each sample adds 40 kg mass and increases fuel burn!
  - **Mission 04: SATELLITE RESCUE (উপগ্রহ মেরামত)** (⭐⭐⭐) — Spacewalk EVA tether maneuver; repair solar array, high-gain dish, and quantum gyro before incoming solar radiation hits!
  - **Mission 05: CREW SURVIVAL (জীবন রক্ষা ও সংকট)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Crisis management; triage between Oxygen leak, Power shortage, and Food contamination.
- **Act 3 — Survival (সংগ্রাম ও সহনশীলতা)**
  - **Mission 06: ASTEROID FIELD (গ্রহাণু বলয়)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Navigate through tumbling space rocks; choose Safe Route vs Risky Route; activate Saiyan Ki-Shield to deflect asteroids!
  - **Mission 07: MARS EXPEDITION (মঙ্গল অভিযান)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Atmospheric descent, Olympus Mons rover exploration, core sample drilling, high power draw, and communication delay.
  - **Mission 08: THE LOST DATA (হারানো ডেটা উদ্ধার)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Dock with abandoned deep-space station near Saturn; reroute power coils, decrypt scientific mainframe data, and make heavy equipment tradeoff decisions.
- **Act 4 — Deep Space (গভীর মহাকাশ)**
  - **Mission 09: DEEP SPACE (গ্যাস দানবদের দেশে)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Outer planet voyage (Uranus & Neptune); execute gravitational slingshot around Jupiter; manage extreme cold (-225°C) and resource scarcity.
- **Final Act — The Climax (চূড়ান্ত অধ্যায়)**
  - **Mission 10: THE FINAL RETURN (ঘরে ফেরার অন্তিম যাত্রা)** (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐) — Multi-crisis gauntlet: emergency fuel leak repair, antenna realignment, solar storm deflection, oxygen balance, and holding atmospheric re-entry corridor (32°–36°) for final touchdown on Earth!

### 2. Decision & Consequence System (PDF Page 11)
- Before every mission, configure your **Cargo Payload**:
  - **Food Supplies (🍖)** & **Water (💧)**: Enhance crew survival and reduce stress.
  - **Ion Propellant Fuel (🚀)**: Powers engines and trajectory maneuvers.
  - **Scientific Equipment (🧪)**: Unlocks massive bonus mission XP.
  - **Oxygen Reserves (🫁)**: Critical life support backup.
- **Consequence Tradeoffs**:
  $$\text{Payload Mass} \uparrow \implies \text{Fuel Consumption Rate} \uparrow \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Maneuverability} \downarrow$$

### 4. Educational Solar System Holo-Deck (From Image Infographics)
Interactive encyclopedia covering all 8 major planets with authentic scientific facts:
- **Mercury (বুধ)**: Smallest, closest to Sun, fastest orbit (~47 km/s), 88 Earth days per year.
- **Venus (শুক্র)**: Hottest (up to 450°C), runaway greenhouse effect, volcanoes, retrograde rotation (spins backwards!).
- **Earth (পৃথিবী)**: Only confirmed life, 70% water surface, 1 moon.
- **Mars (মঙ্গল)**: Red Planet (iron oxide), Phobos & Deimos moons, polar ice caps, extreme weather.
- **Jupiter (বৃহস্পতি)**: Gas giant (H & He), 2× mass of all planets combined, 300+ Earths volume, 80+ moons, Great Red Spot storm.
- **Saturn (শনি)**: Ring system of ice, rocks, and dust; 146 moons (Titan); lowest planetary density.
- **Uranus (ইউরেনাস)**: Coldest (-225°C), 98° axial tilt (spins on its side!), 21-year polar nights, methane blue hue.
- **Neptune (নেপচুন)**: Farthest planet, deep cobalt blue ice giant, 165 Earth years to orbit Sun, 2,100 km/h supersonic winds.
- **Full Chart Viewer**: High-resolution viewer modal for the original planetary infographic sheets.
